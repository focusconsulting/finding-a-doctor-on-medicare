# Finding a doctor on Medicare

A storybook sample by Focus Consulting, November 2026: what people on Medicare, their families and doctors' offices say in public about finding a doctor who takes their plan, and what we would test first.

- Read it: https://focusconsulting.github.io/finding-a-doctor-on-medicare/
- Sources: [sources.html](sources.html)

The storybook draws on 53 public accounts and 451 quotes. Every quote on the page was checked word for word against a saved copy of its source. The two people the story follows are composites, and private people are described by their role, not their name. Counts of accounts show what people mention, not how often anything happens.
